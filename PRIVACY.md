# FlickBoard Privacy Policy

We (FlickBoard) do not track you, however possible.

Word suggestions work entirely offline using bundled Polish and English dictionaries.
By default, personalized learning saves word frequencies and sequences of up to three
words on this device. Polish and English have separate histories. This data is stored
in Android's private no-backup directory; it is not uploaded or included in Android
cloud backups or device transfers. The app does not request network permission.

You can disable suggestions or learning in Settings → Behaviour, switch the suggestion
language, and delete the learned history for both languages with “Clear learned words”.
Disabling learning stops new learning but retains existing history until you clear it.
Passwords, numeric fields, email addresses, URLs and fields explicitly disabling
suggestions are excluded. Editors requesting no personalized learning can use
suggestions without adding to the history. Text surrounding the cursor is used transiently
to find suggestions; moving the cursor or opening an existing document does not train the model.

When you install FlickBoard through a marketplace (such as Google Play), the marketplace will
usually connect that to your marketplace user account, as well as generic identifiers such as your
Android version and device model.

Additionally, Google Play will collect crash logs when the app crashes or freezes, and forward them
to us. This is not something we can control or disable.

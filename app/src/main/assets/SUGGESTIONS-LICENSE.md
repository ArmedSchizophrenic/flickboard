# Bundled suggestion word lists

suggestions-pl.txt and suggestions-en.txt are adapted from the 2018 Polish and
English frequency lists in FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave:

https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/pl/pl_50k.txt
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/en/en_50k.txt

The lists originate from the OpenSubtitles2018 corpus. FrequencyWords licenses
its content under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

These adapted lists are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
Changes: removed occurrence counts, numbers and tokens containing punctuation
other than internal apostrophes; normalized case and apostrophes, deduplicated,
and retained frequency order. No endorsement by the source authors is implied.
